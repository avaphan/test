About Me

Hi, my name is Ava Phan. I am a Marketing student at Iowa State University with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. I am interested in how marketing, technology, artificial intelligence, and business strategy can work together.

Through my coursework and professional experiences, I have developed skills in marketing research, communication, business strategy, artificial intelligence, automation, data analysis, and B2B sales.

My Goals
